Description

A baked potato is a simple and delicious dish that can be enjoyed as a side or a main course. It's made by baking a potato until it's tender and fluffy on the inside, with a crispy skin on the outside. Baked potatoes can be topped with a variety of ingredients, such as butter, sour cream, cheese, bacon, and chives, making them a versatile and satisfying meal option.
