Apple Dump Cake

Home

Description

Apple dump cake is a simple and delicious dessert made with a few basic ingredients. Top it with some vanilla ice cream for an extra treat!

Ingredients

  • 2 cans of apple pie filling
  • 1 box of yellow cake mix
  • 1/2 cup of butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup of chopped nuts (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Pour apple pie filling into a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  3. Sprinkle the cake mix evenly over the pie filling and top it with melted butter. Top with chopped walnuts (optional)
  4. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until the top starts to turn a golden brown.
  5. Let cool 15 minutes before serving.
