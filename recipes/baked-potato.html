Baked Potato

Home

Description

A baked potato is a simple and delicious dish that can be enjoyed as a side or a main course. It's made by baking a potato until it's tender and fluffy on the inside, with a crispy skin on the outside. Baked potatoes can be topped with a variety of ingredients, such as butter, sour cream, cheese, bacon, and chives, making them a versatile and satisfying meal option.

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Optional toppings: butter, sour cream, shredded cheese, bacon bits, chives

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Scrub the potatoes clean and pat them dry with a paper towel.
  3. Prick each potato a few times with a fork to allow steam to escape during baking.
  4. Rub the potatoes with olive oil and sprinkle them with salt.
  5. Place the potatoes directly on the oven rack or on a baking sheet and bake for 45-60 minutes, or until the skin is crispy and the inside is tender when pierced with a fork.
  6. Remove the potatoes from the oven and let them cool for a few minutes before slicing them open and adding your desired toppings.
